Privacy Policy

Effective & updated · 2026-10-29

Your hair portfolio belongs to you. HairGlow keeps projects on your device and does not automatically upload your photos to our servers.

1. What HairGlow does

HairGlow supports offline salon photo editing and optional team sharing. The developer-operated team API runs on Vercel, with Neon PostgreSQL and private Vercel Blob storage in Singapore.

2. Photos and project information

Photos you capture or import, logos, project names, captions, brand settings and edit instructions are stored in the app’s local storage. Original files may contain metadata. We do not automatically send these files to a developer server or supply them as advertising content. Saving to Photos or sharing happens when you choose those actions. The destination app or service then handles the shared copy under its own policy. Device and photo-library backups may also operate according to your Apple, Google and device settings.

On-device analysis and optional team cloud

Face/hair analysis and video creation run on your device. Photos upload only when you choose Upload and confirm. Shared data includes project originals, logos, brand settings, client/stylist details, service, price, booking information and consent records. Authorized members access content according to their roles. We store account email, salted password hashes, hashed sessions and recovery codes, and team permissions. Keep your recovery code safe. Deleting a cloud project or account revokes access immediately; private photo deletion normally completes within 24 hours. Private daily database backups are retained for up to 7 days. Copies downloaded by others require separate deletion. Vercel and Neon process data to provide this service; infrastructure logs may contain request metadata. Purchases are disabled. No user-testing forms or export statistics are collected.

3. Permissions

Camera access supports taking pictures. Photo-library access or the system photo picker supports importing selected photos; save permission supports exporting to Photos where required by the operating system. Screen brightness is adjusted for fill light. This version does not request microphone, contacts or precise-location access. You can change permissions in device settings; denied permissions may limit the related feature.

4. Advertising and privacy choices

HairGlow uses Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). Depending on device settings and consent, Google’s SDK may process IP addresses (including approximate location), advertising/device identifiers, app or ad interactions, and diagnostic/performance information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. This version does not request iOS App Tracking Transparency permission. When required and available, UMP presents privacy choices. Where applicable, use the app’s Ad privacy choices button to revisit them; device advertising settings provide additional controls. Ads are requested only when UMP permits them. No ad availability or choice is required to use the core photo workflow. Read Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps.

5. This website and support

This policy site is hosted by Vercel. Hosting may process request information such as IP address, browser information, URL and time to deliver and secure the site. We add no advertising scripts or analytics cookies to this site. See Vercel’s Privacy Notice. If you contact us, we receive your email address and the information you send, and use them to respond. Please avoid sending customer photos or sensitive information unless necessary.

6. Retention, deletion and backups

Projects remain on your device until you delete them or remove app data. Deleting a project removes its app-managed folder; it does not delete copies exported to Photos, shared elsewhere, or held in device backups. Uninstalling ordinarily removes local app data, subject to operating-system backup and restore behavior. Manage exported copies and backups separately. We cannot remotely access or delete your local photos. Support correspondence is retained as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations. Google and Vercel determine retention for their services under their policies.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, restrict or object to processing, obtain a portable copy, withdraw consent, or complain to a data-protection authority. Use the device/app controls for local data and advertising choices, or contact us for information you sent directly to us. Google and Vercel may process data in other countries as described in their policies. Network security and device safeguards reduce risks but cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Customers, children and updates

Obtain appropriate permission before photographing or sharing someone else’s image. HairGlow is designed primarily for professional portfolios and is not directed at children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has sent personal information to the developer. We may update this policy as features or data practices change; the effective date above identifies the current version.
