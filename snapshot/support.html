Create your first comparison / 製作第一組對比

Create a project, choose a viewing angle, and capture or import Before and After photos. Choose Layout to arrange the comparison, Brand to add your salon name or logo, and Export to save JPG or PNG to Photos or share a copy.

建立作品、選擇拍攝角度，拍攝或匯入 Before 與 After 照片。使用「排版」調整對比方式、「品牌」加入店名及 Logo，最後在「匯出」儲存 JPG、PNG 或分享副本。

Camera and lighting / 拍攝與補光

Allow Camera permission in iOS Settings. Screen lighting is intended for the front camera; rear-camera photography uses supported flash or torch controls. Match After to the translucent Before overlay. Hardware support varies.

請在 iOS 設定允許相機權限。螢幕補光適用於前鏡頭；後鏡頭可使用裝置支援的閃光燈或手電筒。After 拍攝時以半透明 Before 影像手動對齊。功能支援依裝置而異。

Photos, storage and privacy / 照片、空間及隱私

If saving fails, check Photos permission and available storage. Projects stay on this device; export important results before uninstalling. There is no account or developer-operated photo backup. No subscription or purchase is required in version 1.0.

儲存失敗時請檢查相簿權限與可用空間。作品保存在此裝置，卸載前請先匯出重要成品。此版本無帳號或開發者提供的照片備份，1.0 版不需要訂閱或購買。

Privacy policy / 隱私政策 / គោលការណ៍ឯកជនភាព

Report a problem / 回報問題

Please include your device model, iOS version, app version and steps to reproduce. Do not send customer photos or sensitive information unless necessary and you have permission.

請提供裝置型號、iOS 版本、App 版本與重現步驟。非必要請勿傳送顧客照片或敏感資料；傳送前須取得適當許可。
